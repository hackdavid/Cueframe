# Concept board: explain a paper, model or idea by drawing it

The viewer wants to *see* the idea. Draw it on the board step by step while you say it;
never show a page of text.

Shape of the video (one step = one beat, 1-3 spoken sentences):
1. Hook: the question the work answers, as a `section` plus one `text` line.
2. Problem: what was hard before. Prefer a before/after: a `figure` (blank plane,
   x_range [0,10], y_range [0,4]) with two `node`s ("Before: …", "After: …") and a `link`.
3. Key idea: one sentence `quote` from the source, or a `text` line in plain words.
4. How it works: 1-3 steps, each a block diagram on its own wide figure: `node`s for
   inputs, modules and outputs placed left to right (x 1, 4, 7, 9.5; y 1-3), joined by
   `link`s in reading order. Draw nodes in the order you mention them.
5. Results: a `bars` chart with the source's own numbers (same units, labels <= 14
   characters), or `text` lines if there are no numbers. Never invent or round numbers.
6. Why it matters: who it helps, one `text` line.
7. Takeaway: a final `section` "In one line" and one `text`.

Rules:
- Every number, name and claim must come from the source facts given; if the source has no
  result numbers, skip the chart.
- Node labels are 1-4 words (max 28 characters). Diagram text is short; the voice explains.
- Use `write` only for real formulas from the source (LaTeX); no maths checks are needed.
- 6-10 steps. Colours: blue for inputs, purple for the new idea, green for outputs and
  wins, orange for the old way.
- level is "undergrad"; answer is ""; recap has 2-3 takeaways.
