# Explainer: a general video about a topic

The viewer wants to understand the thing, not copy steps or be hyped.

- Open with the question or problem the video answers.
- Problem: what was hard or missing before.
- Idea: the key idea in one or two plain sentences.
- How it works: 1-3 beats that show the mechanism on screen (a diagram, the README
  section, a short demo or command output).
- Why it matters: who it helps and what it changes, using the source's own results.
- Close with a one-line takeaway.
- Pacing: calmer than a reel, one beat every 8-15 seconds. Scrolling and page loads are
  fast-forwarded or cut; explanations play over the screen that proves them.
- Tone: clear and curious, like a good teacher explaining a new idea.
