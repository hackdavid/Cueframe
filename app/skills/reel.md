# Reel / short content: hook, surprising facts, comparison, payoff

Creators don't show everything. The viewer decides in the first 2 seconds whether to
keep watching, so every beat must earn its place.

- Hook (first 2 s): the most surprising true fact or number from the source, said as a
  short punchy line ("4,000 developers starred this in a single day").
- Context in one line: what it is, in words a non-expert gets.
- 1-3 surprising facts, each shown on screen while it's said (the stat, the heading, the
  figure). Numbers beat adjectives.
- A comparison when the source gives one: before vs after, this vs the usual way, a
  benchmark vs a baseline. Never invent the other side of a comparison.
- Fast pacing: one beat every 4-8 seconds. Scrolling, typing and page loads are
  fast-forwarded or cut; only the moment that proves a point plays in real time.
- Payoff: why it matters to the viewer, then a short call to action.
- Tone: energetic, conversational, speaks to "you". Excitement comes from the facts,
  never from claims the source doesn't make.
