Why it matters

Most people learn software from tutorials made for someone else, for another version, about a task that is almost — but not quite — theirs.

01

Tutorials go stale

Menus move and options get renamed. Cueframe plans from the current official docs, then confirms every control against the real UI before recording.

02

Generic isn't your goal

You get only the minimum path to your outcome, at your level — or hand over your files and get the finished result plus how it was done.

03

Nothing is invented

Every step shown was executed and verified. Failed steps are labelled as failed, never faked — so you can trust what you're copying.

Teach me

A tutorial on sample files with "your turn" pauses, option call-outs at decision points and a project file to follow along.

Do it for me

Works on copies of your files, delivers the finished output with a before/after check — and you can change any step afterwards.

Built API-first

The chat is just one client. Every input — messages, images, videos, files — goes through the same REST and WebSocket API, so the agent can be driven from Postman, scripts or MCP, and long jobs keep running when you close the tab.
