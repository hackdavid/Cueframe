Built for three kinds of people

Students & parents

Photograph one question. Cueframe explains what it asks, what's given, how to think about it, the formula, every step and a practice question to try. For a child, a school student or an undergrad.

Creators & dev teams

Tutorials, reels and explainers from a sentence, a link, a PDF or a trending topic. Recorded from a real run, fast-forwarded where it's dull, with captions, hashtags and edits from chat.

Teachers & institutes

Build a library of checked lessons in your syllabus order, share links with students, and let them ask for any question again in their own words.

How it works

  1. 1

    Ask in chat

    A sentence, a photo, a link or a file. Cueframe asks only what it needs: the level, the format, the length.

  2. 2

    It plans, then proves

    Maths is checked line by line with SymPy and reviewed by a second teacher pass. Software is rehearsed in a real cloud sandbox and fixed from what actually appeared on screen.

  3. 3

    You get the video

    Narration, captions, highlights and a thumbnail, right in the chat. Ask for changes: shorter, more energetic, explain step 3 again.
